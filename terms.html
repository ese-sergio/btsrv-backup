Terms of service

BTSRV Backup Service is a private, non-commercial service operated for personal use.

Authorized use

The operator uses the service to back up and restore data they are entitled to manage. This website provides information about the application. It does not accept uploads or offer public accounts.

Availability and recovery

Recovery depends on a usable archive, available storage and access to the destination account. Backup jobs and integrity checks can fail. The operator monitors results and tests restores. No continuous availability or recovery time is guaranteed.

Ending use

The operator can disable scheduled transfers, revoke Google authorization and delete stored copies. See the privacy policy for retention and revocation instructions.
